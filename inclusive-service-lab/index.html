Teaching position

Measure the barrier.

Compare one environmental condition, then redesign it with people who have lived experience.

01 · COMPAREChange one environmental variable.

Keep the task and route constant so students can identify what produced the difference.

02 · MEASURERecord effort, error, time, and choice.

Use force gauges, timers, decibel/lux meters, observation, and participant feedback.

03 · CO-DESIGNLet lived experience judge the redesign.

Invite and compensate older adults and disabled or dementia-community partners whenever possible.
