Method note+

An IAT compares response speed when categories share a response key. This 12-trial version teaches the mechanism; research requires validated stimuli, counterbalanced blocks, and the full D-score algorithm.

How to interpret this comparison+

Read automatic association and stated commitment together—not as competing versions of truth or as a diagnosis of an individual.

Use anonymous class patterns. Never grade individual D-scores.

Why focus on the organization?+

Organizational priorities are learned through performance metrics, routines, leadership behavior, and resource allocation—not only through written policy.

The manuscript found organizational-level discrepancy across all participant groups. Occupational-health effects remain a future research question.

Teaching use + research rationale+

Students audit one event or hotel system, redesign one organizational lever, role-play the revised system, and retest implicit and explicit measures.

Organizational initiatives showed the most consistent implicit–explicit discrepancies across groups (Hua & Yao, manuscript).
